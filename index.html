A Split-Form Discontinuous Galerkin Method
for Spherical Advection on Octahedral Meshes

Student: Yu Sheng, Chiu1, Chi Wei, Wang2, Po Hsun, Liu3  |  Advisor: Prof. Chun-Hao Teng1
1,2,3Department of Applied Mathematics, National Chung Hsing University

Abstract

A split-form discontinuous Galerkin method on octahedral spherical meshes is developed to eliminate pole singularities and discrete chain rule errors. Numerical experiments demonstrate designed high-order accuracy and machine-precision mass conservation ($\sim 10^{-14}$) for spherical transport problems.

1. Introduction

Scalar transport on the sphere is widely used in atmospheric and geophysical applications, while longitude–latitude coordinates suffer from singularities at the poles. To avoid this issue, we adopt an octahedron-based triangular manifold mesh.

Since quadrature-nodal DG discretizations generally do not preserve the continuous chain rule or product rule, a split formulation is introduced to correct the discrete structure of the conservation form. Its numerical behavior is then evaluated through the time evolution of mass and $L_2$ errors.

2. Mathematical Formulation

The mapping conservation law is written as

$$\frac{\partial(Jq)}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial(Ju^r q)}{\partial r} + \frac{\partial(Ju^s q)}{\partial s} = 0.$$

Here, $J$ is the surface Jacobian, while $u^r$ and $u^s$ are the contravariant velocity components.

The mapped conservation law can be rewritten as the split form

$$\begin{aligned} J\frac{\partial q}{\partial t} &+ \frac{1}{2}\Bigg[\frac{\partial(Ju^r q)}{\partial r} + Ju^r\frac{\partial q}{\partial r} + q\frac{\partial(Ju^r)}{\partial r} \\ &\quad + \frac{\partial(Ju^s q)}{\partial s} + Ju^s\frac{\partial q}{\partial s} + q\frac{\partial(Ju^s)}{\partial s}\Bigg] = 0. \end{aligned}$$

4. Discrete Formulation

Quadrature-Nodal Operators
$$V_{ij}=\phi_j(r_i,s_i), \quad (V_b^\gamma)_{ij} = \phi_j(r_i^\gamma,s_i^\gamma), \quad (V_m)_{ij}=\frac{\partial\phi_j(r_i^\gamma,s_i^\gamma)}{\partial m}, \quad P=(V^T W V)^{-1}V^T W$$
$$D_r=V_rP, \quad D_s=V_sP, \quad R^\gamma=V_b^\gamma P, \quad L^\gamma = \frac{1}{J} W^{-1} (R^\gamma)^T W_b$$
Split-Form DG Discretization
$$\begin{aligned} \frac{d\mathbf q}{dt} ={}& -\frac{1}{2J} \Big[ D_r\big((Ju^r)\odot\mathbf q\big) + (Ju^r)\odot D_r\mathbf q + \mathbf q\odot D_r(Ju^r) \\ &+ D_s\big((Ju^s)\odot\mathbf q\big) + (Ju^s)\odot D_s\mathbf q + \mathbf q\odot D_s(Ju^s) \Big] + \sum_{\gamma\subset\partial T} L^\gamma p^\gamma \end{aligned}$$
$$p^\gamma = \frac{1}{2} \bigg[n_r^\gamma R^\gamma(J u^r \odot \mathbf{q}) + n_s^\gamma R^\gamma(J u^s \odot \mathbf{q}) + [n_r^\gamma R^\gamma(J u^r) + n_s^\gamma R^\gamma(J u^s) ] \odot (R^\gamma \mathbf{q})\bigg] - F^{*,\gamma}$$
Time Integration
$$\frac{d\mathbf q}{dt} = \mathcal{R}(\mathbf q,t), \qquad \mathbf{\text{LSRK54 : 5-stage, 4th-order}}$$
